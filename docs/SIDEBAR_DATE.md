# Side Panel: Deadline Date

## What is it?

The **date** in the side panel (when viewing a brief) is the **Deadline** field. It appears in the Properties section under "Properties".

## Why is it necessary?

- **Prioritization**: The team uses it to know when work is due and in what order to tackle briefs.
- **Tracking**: Helps pod leads and admins see which briefs are at risk of missing their due date.
- **Context**: When a client or stakeholder has a hard deadline, this is where it’s recorded.

## When is it used?

- Pod members and leads can set or change the deadline when a brief is claimed or in progress.
- The date is optional; not every brief needs one.
- It’s only visible in the internal (pod) view, not on the client side.
